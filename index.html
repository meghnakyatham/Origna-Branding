The Import Pipeline

WHAT ORIGNA ACTUALLY DOES

01 / DISCOVER

We search beyond the obvious.

Our curation team identifies exceptional international makers, focusing on those who refuse to compromise on quality and maintain clear regional heritage.

02 / SELECT

Every product earns its place.

We test hundreds of chocolates, coffees, and spreads. Only products with exceptional flavor profiles and pure ingredients join the ORIGNA collection.

03 / VERIFY

Clear provenance & authenticity.

We verify the production origins, organic certifications, and fair-trade documentation directly with the makers. No counterfeit, no gray market.

04 / IMPORT

Cold-chain international transit.

We directly manage the importation from European, American, and Japanese makers into India, avoiding third-party distributors who break handling standards.

05 / PROTECT

Temperature-controlled storage.

Fine chocolate and coffee are delicate. We handle, store, and warehouse our entire collection under precise, temperature-controlled environments in India.

06 / DELIVER

From the origin to your doorstep.

Packaged in thermal insulated boxes with cold packs, we deliver the world's most delicate gourmet products directly to homes across India in perfect condition.

Trust System

BEFORE IT REACHES YOU, IT PASSES THROUGH ORIGNA.

01 / SOURCE

Where did it come from?

We source directly from licensed brand owners and original chocolate kitchens in Europe and Japan. Every batch is certified with official certificates of origin.

02 / AUTHENTICITY

Is it genuine?

We guarantee 100% genuine product formulations. Unlike gray-market sellers, Origna does not deal in near-expiry items or repackaged counterfeit goods.

03 / QUALITY

How was it handled?

Fine chocolate easily ruins if exposed to heat. Our cold-chain shipping maintains a strict temperature between 16°C and 19°C from the maker's cellar to our Indian hubs.

04 / ORIGIN

Who made it?

We provide full documentation and background on every chocolatier and roaster. We believe knowing the artisan makes the tasting experience more meaningful.

05 / DELIVERY

How does it reach you?

Every shipment is packaged in thermal foil liners with dry ice packs, guaranteeing your delicate chocolates arrive in solid, pristine bar form, even in peak Indian summer.
